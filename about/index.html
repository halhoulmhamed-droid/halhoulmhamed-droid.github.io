About A mathematical route into software

Engineering informed by structure, not spectacle.

I work at the intersection of Python backend systems, API integration, AI automation, and applied mathematics. That path is an extension of how I have worked for years: make the assumptions visible, reason carefully, and communicate the result clearly.

01 Background

xf(x)

From mathematics education to dependable software systems.

I hold a Licence d’Études Fondamentales in Sciences Mathématiques et Applications from Université Ibn Zohr. I am currently in the second year of the Master’s program in Modélisation, Mathématiques Appliquées et Apprentissage at Abdelmalek Essaâdi University.

Alongside that academic path, I bring more than a decade of experience teaching mathematics in Moroccan upper-secondary education.

My current technical work focuses on Python and AI systems, LLM integration, backend services, AI automation, and reliable AI applications. I am also developing deeper practical experience with retrieval-augmented generation, MCP-based tool integration, and AI agent architectures.

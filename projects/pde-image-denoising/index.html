Academic study A1 Numerical PDEs

Image denoising with PDEs

Published M2 course project

A reproducible comparison of linear heat diffusion and two directional Perona–Malik schemes, focused on the trade-off between noise reduction and edge preservation.

01

Question

Noise reduction and edge preservation.

Under what conditions does gradient-dependent diffusion change the trade-off between noise reduction and edge preservation compared with linear heat diffusion?

The study uses two deterministic 128 × 128 synthetic images, Gaussian noise, and shared noisy observations across methods. A recorded parameter grid varies conduction, its threshold, and diffusion time.

02

Methods

A linear baseline and two directional schemes.

  • 01
    Linear heat diffusion

    A numerical baseline for spatial smoothing.

  • 02
    Perona–Malik diffusion

    Exponential and rational conduction in a four-neighbour directional discretisation.

  • 03
    Controlled evaluation

    PSNR and SSIM at shared iteration checkpoints, with means and sample standard deviations across ten noise seeds.

The Python implementation uses NumPy float64 on CPU. Numerical arrays remain unclipped; clipping is used only for image display.

03

Recorded findings

The metric and parameters matter.

The extended study archives 60 shared noisy observations, 540 solver trajectories, and 4,380 metric rows. In the fixed geometric-shapes example at noise level 0.10, iteration 20, and conduction threshold 0.10, exponential Perona–Malik gives higher mean PSNR than heat diffusion but lower SSIM.

The recorded grid also includes settings that retain noise or oversmooth the image. The conduction function, threshold, diffusion time, and evaluation criterion must be considered together; the examples do not establish a generally superior method.

04

Limitations

Study scope Conditional results

Two synthetic images define the evidence.

The project is educational and makes no research-novelty claim. It does not evaluate natural images or deep-learning methods, and it is not a general image benchmark.

Ten noise seeds are repeated observations of the same two images. The plotted bands are sample standard deviations, not confidence intervals. Best settings selected with access to the clean reference are exploratory oracles, not deployable tuning rules.

Properties of the implemented directional scheme do not establish well-posedness of the classical continuous Perona–Malik model.

05

Materials

Code, results, and reading material.

The public MIT-licensed repository includes numerical solvers, tests, frozen configurations, archived arrays and metrics, scientific figures, mathematical references, and LaTeX sources. Its README provides reproduction instructions that use separate output directories.

The French report and presentation state the complete academic affiliation and credit Mhamed Halhoul and Khalil Amraoui. Their figures and numerical results come from the archived study.

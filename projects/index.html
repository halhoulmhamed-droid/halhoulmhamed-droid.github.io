Image Denoising with PDEs

Noise reduction and edge preservation

A comparison of linear heat diffusion and two four-neighbour directional Perona–Malik schemes, evaluated with PSNR and SSIM across a recorded parameter study.

Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Larache · M2 in progress, Master Modélisation mathématique et applications et apprentissages. Module: Modélisation déterministe et applications. Enseignant du module : Pr. Hatim Tayeq. Report and slides: Mhamed Halhoul and Khalil Amraoui.

  • Numerical PDEs
  • Python
  • NumPy
  • Reproducibility

Scope: two synthetic images, three noise levels, and ten noise seeds. The results are conditional on this study.
